# Objective: Apply client-requested fixes and process changes to the GCE vehicle buying and sales system

## Role

Full-stack developer maintaining the GCE vehicle buying and sales system.

## Goal

Implement the client's latest feedback: new process rules, feature changes, removals, refactors, and bug fixes across the Buyer, Sales Manager, Head Accountant, and Confidential Informant modules.

- Add work schedules for attendance by role.
- Automate payment recording and stop duplicate submissions.
- Add a vehicle-papers workflow for the Confidential Informant.
- Fix the "Unauthorized" error in the Confidential Informant dashboard.

## Success Criteria

- Every task below is built and works as described.
- Record Payment fills in its data automatically and accepts only one submission per transaction for CASH (GCE Visit, Halfway, Delivery).
- A buyer cannot submit a buying request without proof of billing.
- Opening a delivery under Field Cases in `confidential_informant/dashboard` no longer shows "Unauthorized".

## Constraints

- The Sales Manager changes to buying requests and the Record Payment automation apply to these CASH payment options: GCE Visit, Halfway, and Delivery.
- The Confidential Informant prints only the Deed of Sale and the Policy &amp; Warranty. The OR/CR is not printed; it is the original physical document.
- Delivery is free within CALABARZON.
- The down payment is 10% of the vehicle price.

## Context

- GCE buys vehicles from suppliers and from clients who sell to GCE, then resells them to buyers.
- Roles in the system: Buyer, Sales Manager, Head Accountant, Account Manager, Marketing Specialist, Confidential Informant, and Mechanic.
- The original client feedback was in Filipino. The Confidential Informant requirements come from the client's own written request. The developer's notes from a video match it: a 3-dot menu reveals the requested papers, and only the Deed of Sale and Policy &amp; Warranty are printable.

### Buyer: buying request

- Fix the loophole that lets a buyer submit a request without proof of billing. Proof of billing must be required.
- Let the buyer remove an uploaded image if they submitted the wrong one.
- Require both the front and back of the ID.

### Sales Manager: buying requests (CASH: GCE Visit, Halfway, Delivery)

- Remove the "Cancel" option from the Sales Manager's decision actions.
- Remove the Sales Manager's ability to add documents or requirements to the buyer's request.
- Remove the "Cancel" option after the Sales Manager approves the request.
- Once the Sales Manager requests papers from the Confidential Informant, remove both "Cancel" and "Buyer Declined."

### Record Payment (CASH: GCE Visit, Halfway, Delivery)

- Automate Record Payment so its data is fetched automatically.
- Allow only one submission, so the record can't be spammed.

### Head Accountant

- Remove the "Mark Sold" action.
- Remove the Record Payment action.
- Restyle the button at the bottom of the payment record so it looks like a button.

### Confidential Informant: vehicle papers

- Add a new sidebar tab for vehicle papers: OR/CR, Deed of Sale, and Policy &amp; Warranty.
- In this tab, show the items that currently appear in the existing process.
- Add a 3-dot menu to each item. Clicking it opens the paper request and shows the OR/CR, Deed of Sale, and Policy &amp; Warranty fetched from GCE's records.
- Make the Deed of Sale and Policy &amp; Warranty printable.
- Auto-fill the Deed of Sale with the fetched data. The buyer name on it is GCE's current buyer.
- The Confidential Informant hands over the physical OR/CR they kept from when GCE bought the car from the supplier or selling client.
- Once all papers are given to the Sales Manager, the Confidential Informant marks the papers as "Released" in their account.

### Sales Manager: customer profile and pricing

- In the customer profile, add dropdowns for Region, Province, City, and Barangay, plus a free-text field for Block and Lot.
- Show delivery fee ranges by area:
  - CALABARZON: free
  - Luzon: ₱8,000 – ₱10,000
  - Visayas: ₱10,000 – ₱15,000
  - Mindanao: ₱15,000 – ₱20,000
- Show the delivery fee range to the buyer. Show it in the Sales Manager's DSS too, with the label "Price Range" beside the delivery fee.
- Set the down payment to 10% of the vehicle price.

### Sales Manager: transactions (`sales_manager/transaction`)

- Remove the Mechanic from deliveries.
- Remove the option to bring a Mechanic along on a delivery.

### Bug fix: Confidential Informant dashboard

- Under Field Cases in `confidential_informant/dashboard`, opening a delivery shows "Unauthorized." Fix it.
