# Objective: Implement GCE Client Change Requests (Batch 2)

## Role

Full-stack web developer working on the Global Car Exchange (GCE) system's customer flows, payments, authentication, and employee management (attendance, payroll, leave).

## Goal

Implement the second batch of client-requested changes from the review clips and additional notes. These cover the selling flow, payments, a new landing page, authentication fixes, and employee attendance and payroll.

- Fix the reported bugs: file and image sending in chat, the show-password toggle, Continue with Google, registration verification, and Bank Transfer with a downpayment.
- Add attendance, hours-based payroll, overtime, salary deductions, and leave requests for employees.

## Success Criteria

- Every item in the Tasks section below is implemented and checked off.
- Payroll is computed from each employee's hours on duty, with overtime hours tracked separately and paid at a higher rate.
- Financing adds 10% to the car's price and offers periods of 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, 3 years, and 5 years.
- Bank Transfer works when the customer makes a downpayment.

## Constraints

- If the arrangement type is "GCE Visit", the location field must not be required.
- A customer login page is optional because it was not required in Thesis A. If one is built, the customer reaches it by clicking from the landing page.
- Employee working schedules are still to be sent by the client. Attendance work depends on them.

## Context

- Source: the client's notes and summaries from three review clips plus two additional notes.
- The client compared the selling flow against the version last submitted.
- "After Selling" refers to the process of posting the vehicle.
- Leave requests go to the Accountant Manager.
- Salary deductions were not shown in the clips, but the client asked for them to be included.
