# Account Manager Audit Report

> User Level: Account Manager - Investigation #2

## Summary

The Account Manager has access to 19 staff modules covering dashboards, operations, staff management, and admin. This scope matches a coordinator role that links customers, suppliers, transactions, field cases, and staff support. Most modules are needed for day-to-day coordination; two admin-detail areas are broader than daily work and should be narrowed to a summary or restricted view.

## Modules Audited

### 1. Dashboard (Default) — Dashboards
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Shows a daily snapshot of metrics, performance, reconditioning, and alerts to prioritize accounts and tasks.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — needs a single starting view to plan follow-ups across customers, suppliers, and staff.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate — read-only overview, no extra steps.
- **Recommendation:** Keep — preserve as the daily starting point.

### 2. Finance — Dashboards
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Tracks financial entries, disbursement requests, and purchase-fund requests tied to transactions.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — handles day-to-day money requests and purchase funding for vehicles before final approval.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate — Account Manager can record and request, while verification and advancing remain with CEO and Head Accountant, which keeps separation of duties.
- **Recommendation:** Keep — current permission limits are correct; no broader access needed.

### 3. Vehicles — Operations
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Lists the full vehicle inventory with condition, stock code, and pricing status.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — needs visibility to answer account questions, coordinate with sales, and align supplier sourcing.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate — viewing and coordination, not primary pricing or listing work.
- **Recommendation:** Keep — keep visibility; leave pricing approvals and listing edits to Sales/Marketing and CEO.

### 4. Inspections — Operations
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Shows mechanic inspection reports and checklist results for each vehicle.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — needs to confirm a vehicle is ready before promising delivery or closing an account issue.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate — status tracking without requiring the Account Manager to perform inspections.
- **Recommendation:** Keep — preserve read and follow-up access.

### 5. Inquiries — Operations
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Holds customer inquiry chats, including vehicle-linked questions and account follow-ups.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — core to customer and account relations; Account Manager is a primary responder alongside Sales.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate — filtered chat with reply and handoff; volume is the only pressure point.
- **Recommendation:** Keep — keep response access; use assignment filters so only account-related inquiries route to this role by default.

### 6. Recommendations — Operations
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Displays inventory allocation, sales overview, market activity, and suggested matches for customers.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — helps match customers to available stock and explain demand trends.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate — overview dashboards, not daily editing.
- **Recommendation:** Keep — keep as an insight and allocation aid.

### 7. Transactions — Operations
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Tracks all customer purchase, sell, and request transactions, states, and linked details.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — central to closing deals, handling buy/sell requests, and resolving account issues.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate — filtering by state and full history supports coordination and audit.
- **Recommendation:** Keep — keep full coordination access.

### 8. Roadmap — Operations
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Shows company plans, projects, performance highlights, and upcoming milestones.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — for awareness, so account and supplier work aligns with strategy.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate — view-only for Account Manager; only CEO can edit, which is correct.
- **Recommendation:** Keep — preserve read access; no edit needed for this role.

### 9. Suppliers — Operations
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Manages supplier records, contact details, documents, and approval status.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — Account Manager is the day-to-day owner with CEO for inviting, collecting documents, and following up; supplier quality directly affects inventory.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate — invite, verify, approve or reject in two steps.
- **Recommendation:** Keep — keep shared ownership with CEO; Account Manager handles collection, CEO retains final approval.

### 10. Staff Records — Staff Management
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Holds staff profiles, work schedules, performance reviews, and walk-in registration.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — needed to coordinate staffing, schedules, and basic people support.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate — viewing and coordination; sensitive performance detail should not be the daily view.
- **Recommendation:** Keep — keep access; show summary performance by default and require extra step for detailed reviews.

### 11. Attendance — Staff Management
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Shows clock-ins, clock-outs, hours, and attendance status for the team.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — needs to see who is available to assign customer and field work.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate for checkers, but full logs are heavy if reviewed every day.
- **Recommendation:** Simplify — give a daily summary and exception alerts; keep detailed per-person logs behind a drill-down.

### 12. Employee Requests — Staff Management
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Handles formal staff requests such as leave and other approvals.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — Account Manager is a first-line reviewer with CEO for staff support.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate — submit, review, approve or reject with clear ownership.
- **Recommendation:** Keep — keep reviewer role; filter so only team-relevant or escalated requests reach this role.

### 13. Payroll — Staff Management
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Shows payroll runs and total compensation before payout.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — helps prepare and check runs with Head Accountant before CEO sign-off.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate — review and prepare, not sole approval.
- **Recommendation:** Keep — keep preparation and review; leave final sign-off with CEO and verification with Head Accountant.

### 14. Payslips — Staff Management
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Lists individual payslips per employee and pay period.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** No — individual payslip detail is not needed for daily coordination; payroll summary and own payslip are enough.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate for Finance, but overly granular for daily Account Manager work.
- **Recommendation:** Consider for Revision — limit to summary and exception view plus own payslip; keep full individual access with Head Accountant and CEO for audit. Retain broader access only when audit requires it.

### 15. Field Cases — Staff Management
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Tracks off-site cases such as repossessions and field investigations, with assignment and status.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — Account Manager coordinates between customers, transactions, and field workers when cases affect accounts.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate — monitor and coordinate; creation and field execution stay with CEO, Confidential Informant, and Sales/Mechanic workers.
- **Recommendation:** Keep — keep coordinator visibility and assignment view.

### 16. Reports — Staff Management
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Stores formal business reports by kind and period for review and archive.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — creates operational and account reports for leadership review.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate — Account Manager can create and submit; review stays with CEO and Head Accountant.
- **Recommendation:** Keep — preserve creation and submission; no review-authority change needed.

### 17. Announcements — Staff Management
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Shares company-wide messages with staff, with publish and expiry controls.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — for staying informed and keeping the team aligned.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate — view of published announcements is correct; creation and publish remain with CEO.
- **Recommendation:** Keep — keep as view-only for Account Manager; no publish needed.

### 18. Roles — Pages / Admin
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Defines roles, permission combinations, and which groups each role can do.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Partially — helps with onboarding and troubleshooting access, but defining permissions is a sensitive admin function.
- **Workflow:** Overly Complex for daily Account Manager work if expected to create or edit permission definitions; protects system access and should have strong guardrails.
- **Recommendation:** Consider for Revision — keep view access, but limit edit to user assignment or require CEO approval and audit trail for creating permissions or changing protected roles.

### 19. Users — Pages / Admin
- **What it does for Account Manager:** Manages user accounts — inviting, viewing state, and updating roles and account status.
- **Necessary for Account Manager?** Yes — delegated admin for day-to-day onboarding and account support alongside CEO.
- **Workflow:** Appropriate — centralized control with shared ownership.
- **Recommendation:** Keep — keep routine invite and assignment with Account Manager; keep sensitive suspend or archive actions with approval and audit, CEO retains final control.

## Overall Recommendations

- **Keep as is:** Dashboard, Finance, Vehicles, Inspections, Inquiries, Recommendations, Transactions, Roadmap, Suppliers, Staff Records, Employee Requests, Payroll, Field Cases, Reports, Announcements, Users
- **Simplify:** Attendance — daily summary and exception view for Account Manager; detailed logs remain available on drill-down. Ensure Staff Records shows summary performance by default.
- **Consider for Revision/Removal:** Payslips — revise to summary-only plus own payslip for Account Manager; full browse stays with Finance and CEO for audit. Roles — revise to view plus limited user-assignment or approval-gated edits; do not give routine permission-definition to Account Manager. No module is recommended for full removal.

## Notes

- All recommendations are suggestions only — no functionality has been deleted. Approvals, separation of duties, and audit trails should be preserved when simplifying or revising views.
- Why the current set fits: Account Manager bridges customers (Inquiries, Transactions, Recommendations, Vehicles, Inspections), suppliers (Suppliers), field operations (Field Cases), money flow (Finance, Payroll, Reports), and people coordination (Staff Records, Attendance, Employee Requests, Users). Showroom and Content are appropriately absent — they belong to Marketing Specialist for daily editing; Security Duty Checks is appropriately absent — it belongs to Head of Security; Supplier Messages is appropriately absent — direct supplier threads stay with CEO for sensitive negotiation, while Account Manager handles supplier onboarding and documents in Suppliers. This separation avoids duplicate inboxes and keeps security and marketing in specialist hands.
- Excessive or missing: Roles feels excessive at full edit level; Payslips feels excessive for daily use and should be trimmed. Attendance could be lighter with a summary-first view. No major module is missing — if supplier chat moves to Supplier Messages in the future, consider giving Account Manager view or delegated thread access rather than full ownership.
- Evidence: evaluated 19 modules from `src/lib/auth/roles.ts` (`ROLE_NAV_ACCESS.account_manager`) and group order from `src/navigation/sidebar/sidebar-items.ts`; cross-checked with `docs/report/REPORT-CEO.md` style; spot-checked `src/app/(staff)/` folders and pages for finance, suppliers, vehicles, inspections, inquiries, recommendations, transactions, roadmap, staff-records, attendance, employee-requests, payroll, payslips, field-cases, reports, announcements, roles, and users. Workflow notes are based on code-level role guards and components, not live user testing — confirm simplifications with Account Manager and HR/Finance owners before changing views.
