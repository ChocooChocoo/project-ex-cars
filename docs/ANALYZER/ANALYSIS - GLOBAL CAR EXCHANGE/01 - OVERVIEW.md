## What it is

Global Car Exchange is described as a proposed online system that brings vehicle sales work, customer conversations, records, recommendations, payroll, approvals, and management information into one place. Customers would use it through web pages that also fit phones. Staff would use the same shared information instead of separate spreadsheets, paper records, and scattered conversations. — **GCE FULL CHAPTER 1 - 3.docx**, pages 10–17; **GCE USERS LEVELS MODULES.md**, headings “CEO” through “Head Security”

Its three named centrepieces are a Virtual Showroom for viewing vehicles, a Decision Support System for helping decisions, and a mobile-friendly experience. — **GCE FULL CHAPTER 1 - 3.docx**, pages 11–16

## What it does

The proposed customer journey starts with a landing page and a searchable vehicle list. A person can register, verify contact details, complete a profile, save favourite cars, view a vehicle in 360 degrees, and start an inquiry tied to that vehicle. — **GCE ADDITIONAL DOCUMENTS.docx**, pages 1–2

The proposed recommendation tool compares a customer's budget and preferences with the available vehicles. The additional document gives fixed weights for budget, condition, fuel efficiency, resale or demand value, and mileage. — **GCE ADDITIONAL DOCUMENTS.docx**, pages 2–3

Three transaction paths are named: buying a listed vehicle, selling a vehicle to Global Car Exchange, and asking the company to find a vehicle that is not listed. Each path is meant to show progress from pending, through review, to approval or rejection and then completion. — **GCE ADDITIONAL DOCUMENTS.docx**, page 2

Behind the customer pages, staff are meant to share live inventory, customer conversations, inspection records, transaction records, attendance requests, basic financial records, management reports, and marketing content. — **GCE FULL CHAPTER 1 - 3.docx**, pages 15–21; **GCE ADDITIONAL DOCUMENTS.docx**, pages 2–3

The newest role material adds a clear inquiry-to-sales handoff. Inquiry actions go to the Account Manager, while Buy Now actions go to the Sales Manager. Registration details fill later forms automatically. A client can choose delivery, a meet-up within CALABARZON, or a visit to GCE. Walk-in buyers and sellers can have an account created by the Sales Manager, and a purchasing client must still provide two valid IDs and proof of billing. — **GCE USERS LEVELS MODULES.md**, headings “Account Manager — 2. Inquiries and Viewing Schedule” and “Sales Manager — 1. Buy Now and Inquiry Handling” through “Sales Manager — 4. Walk-In Client Selling Their Own Vehicle”

Payroll is now part of the proposed work. Attendance, leave, overtime, late arrivals, salary amounts, and stated deductions feed a payroll report. The Account Manager prepares it, while the Head Accountant has payslip and salary-payment responsibilities. The documents do not settle the final approval order. — **GCE USERS LEVELS MODULES.md**, headings “Account Manager — 4. Attendance, Leave, and Overtime Records; Payroll Processing; and Reconditioning Disbursements” and “Head Accountant — 2. Payroll”

Vehicle-purchase funds, reconditioning disbursements, installment follow-up, repossession assignments, vehicle repair progress, sales paperwork, announcements, and security photographs also become named staff work. — **GCE USERS LEVELS MODULES.md**, headings “CEO — 2. Approval and Rejection of Reports” through “Head Security — 2. Proof of Duty”

The chapter document also describes planned pages for browsing, vehicle details, favourites, messages, notifications, and profile changes. These descriptions name working-file labels, but no source code was supplied to prove that the pages exist. — **GCE FULL CHAPTER 1 - 3.docx**, pages 33–43

The revision list adds work in every area. Suppliers register as a Company or an Individual, present two primary valid IDs, and wait for GCE approval before their first sign-in; the Procurement Team is meant to create the account rather than leaving the supplier to register alone. Chat carries photographs and files, offers a way to report a message, and hides bad language by itself. Vehicle listings gain warranty details, promotional offers, condition, and a Negotiable or Fixed pricing type. A checklist covers the required vehicle documents. The mechanic's checklist becomes nested vehicle systems, components, and parts; marking a part for repair or replacement opens fields for the replacement item name, brand, and estimated cost; and the inspection report then reuses those answers instead of asking again. Forms fill themselves from stored details and check every field. Submissions, approvals, and deletions ask for confirmation, and light-mode text needs stronger contrast. — **REVISIONS LISTS.md**, headings “1. Chat, Notifications, and Reports” through “14. Submission, Approval, and Deletion Confirmation”

## What state it is in

The material consistently calls this a “proposed” system and says it “will” be developed. The newest file describes responsibilities but does not supply working files. The safest status is therefore ❓ Unclear for every build item, even where the documents describe detailed page behaviour. — **GCE FULL CHAPTER 1 - 3.docx**, pages 11–17 and 32–45; **GCE USERS LEVELS MODULES.md**, headings “CEO” through “Head Security”

The written study itself is incomplete. Its approval sheet still contains sample names, its acknowledgement, dedication, and abstract contain placeholder text, its login picture is a generic example, its data-flow picture refers to residents rather than car exchange work, and its architecture page contains a dummy image. — **GCE FULL CHAPTER 1 - 3.docx**, pages 2–5, 33, 44, and 45

## What it does not do

The stated boundary excludes direct online payment processing, a payment gateway, live delivery tracking, automatic logistics coordination, scheduled social-media posting, email campaign management, automatic marketplace listing, offline use, advanced benefits administration, and full-scale accounting. Human delivery assignments, meet-ups, visits, and vehicle recovery are still included. Payroll is included by the current authority. Supplier registration and approval have returned to the plan through the revision list. Native iOS and Android applications were excluded, but the revision list now asks for an in-system application download, so that boundary is contested rather than settled. — **GCE FULL CHAPTER 1 - 3.docx**, pages 16–17; **GCE USERS LEVELS MODULES.md**, headings “CEO” through “Head Security”; **REVISIONS LISTS.md**, headings “5. Supplier Registration and Account Management” and “7. Mobile Application Download”
