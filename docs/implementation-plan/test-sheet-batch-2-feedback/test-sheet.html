15. Payslips by hours, overtime and deductions

Client answer: Works (Saved Oct 30, 2026, 5:43 AM by Cesar Bonifacio)

Before

Pay was counted by days present. Overtime and lateness were not shown.

Now

Pay is counted from hours worked. Approved overtime is paid at 125% on its own line. Minutes late or leaving early are one deduction line. SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG are still deducted, and you can add items such as a cash advance.

Do thisYou should seeDev reply
1 Sign in as the Account Manager. Open Payroll, press Create Run and pick a period with checked attendance. "Payroll run created from checked attendance." Draft payslips appear.
2 Open one payslip. Regular Hours and Overtime Hours at the top of the Summary.
3 Look at the lines. Regular hours, Overtime hours (only with an approved overtime request) and Late / undertime, each with a note such as "90.00 h × ₱74.07/h". Each amount matches its note.
4 Open the payslip of someone who worked extra hours with no approved overtime request. No Overtime hours line.
5 On a draft payslip, press Add Item and add a deduction, for example Cash advance ₱500. The new line shows and the net pay goes down by ₱500.

Note: The 125% overtime rate and the deductions are for the demo. An accountant should confirm them before GCE pays real salaries.
